\subsection{Legendre Polynomials}
Let $P_k(x)$ be the degree $k$ Legendre polynomial. The Legendre polynomials
are an orthogonal family of polynomials on $[-1,1]$ that obey the orthogonality
relation
$$
\int_{-1}^1 P_j(x) P_k(x) dx = 0,
$$
for $j \not = k$. The first few Legendre polynomials are
$$
P_0(x) = 1, \qquad P_1(x) = x, \qquad \text{and} \qquad P_2(x) = \frac{3 x^2 - 1}{2}.  
$$
The Legendre polynomials obey the recurrence relation
$$
(k+1) P_{k+1}(x) = (2 k + 1) x P_k(x) - k P_{k-1}(x),
$$
the differential equation
$$
(1 - x^2) P_k''(x) - 2 x P_k'(x) + k(k+1) P_k(x) = 0,
$$
for $k \ge 1$.
