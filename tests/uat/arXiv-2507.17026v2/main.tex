\begin{abstract}
We study the $\cM$-closed validation of a generative model $q$ against a
target $p$ over the space $\cX$. Conformal calibration turns a weak plug-in
classifier into a powerful two-sample test with finite-sample type-I control.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
A popular tool for evaluating generative models is the classifier two-sample
test (C2ST). It reframes distributional comparison between $p(x)$ and $q(x)$ as
a classification task over the space $\cX$, with the Bayes-optimal classifier
bisecting $\RR^2$ between the two means.

\paragraph{Summary of contributions.} We introduce the Conformal C2ST and give
a robustness theory for weak classifiers. We show that conformal calibration
decouples type-I error control from discriminative accuracy.

\paragraph{A toy example.} Consider $\cN(\mathbf{0}, I_2)$ versus a mean shift,
so that $q$ is $\cN([0.5, 0]^\top, I_2)$. The rank statistic
\[
  r = \sum_{i=1}^{M} \bI\{\theta \le \tilde\theta_i\}
\]
is uniform over $\{0, 1, \dots, M\}$ when $q$ matches $p$.

\section{Robustness of the uniform test}
Let $\cO$ denote an oracle and write the conformal $p$-value as a function of
the score. We control the variance $\var[r]$ and the expectation $\EE[r]$ of
the rank statistic under the null.

\begin{theorem}[Decoupling]
\label{thm:decouple}
For any plug-in classifier with score $s$, the conformal calibration map
satisfies
\[
  \Big| \EE\big[ \phi(s) \big] - \alpha \Big| \le \norm{s - s^\star}
  \xrightarrow{\;n \to \infty\;} 0 ,
\]
where $s^\star$ is the Bayes-optimal score and $\abs{\alpha} \le 1$.
\end{theorem}

As shown in Theorem~\ref{thm:decouple}, the conformal test maintains validity
even when the classifier is weak, since the ranking signal
$\overset{\text{rank}}{\longrightarrow}$ aggregates across draws.
